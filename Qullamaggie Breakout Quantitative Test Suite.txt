Quantifying Momentum Breakout Dynamics: Institutional Verification Engine and Algorithmic Test Suite for Qullamaggie Setups
High-frequency and quantitative trading systems require exact mathematical definitions to separate structural market edges from generic technical noise. The momentum breakout strategy popularized by Kristjan Kullamägi (Qullamaggie) captures explosive, multi-day to multi-week trend continuations1. The strategy identifies equities that have demonstrated extreme prior momentum, consolidated within a tightening price structure, and broken out above horizontal resistance pivots while remaining supported by short-term exponential moving averages2.
Generic technical stock screeners generate excessive false positives because they rely on static indicators, such as simple moving average crossovers or standard Relative Strength Index bounds. Translating the Qullamaggie Breakout (BO) setup into an institutional-grade automated pipeline requires formalizing the setup through multi-timeframe geometric metrics, dynamic volatility calculations, and deterministic algorithmic rules. This report provides a quantitative formalization of the setup, a low-latency screening architecture designed to minimize computational overhead, and an automated verification test suite to validate signal fidelity.
Quantitative Formalization of the Qullamaggie Breakout Setup
The Qullamaggie breakout methodology targets equities undergoing structural consolidation following a primary impulse move2. Market microstructure studies reveal that during the initial advance, aggressive institutional buying absorbs floating supply1. The subsequent consolidation represents a period of inventory digestion, where profit-taking is met with sustained buying interest rather than aggressive distribution3.
